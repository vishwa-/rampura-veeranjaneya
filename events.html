Jeernoddhara Maha Kumbhabhishekam Featured

and the Prathisthapana of Sri Vidya Hayagreeva Swamy

Friday – Sunday · July 3 – 5, 2026·Rampura

The three-day consecration of the reborn Sri Anjaneya Swamy Devasthanam and the installation of Sri Vidya Hayagreeva. Under the guidance of Pancharatra Agama Praveena Vidwan Sri B.R. Gopal Bhattar; organized by the Devatha family. Maha Annadana follows the ceremonies — the entire Rampura gramastharu and all devotees are welcome.
