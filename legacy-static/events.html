Jul
3–5

Jeernoddhara Maha Kumbhabhishekam Sampoorna

and the Prathisthapana of Sri Vidya Hayagreeva Swamy

Friday – Sunday · July 3 – 5, 2026·Rampura

The three-day consecration of the reborn Sri Anjaneya Swamy Devasthanam and the installation of Sri Vidya Hayagreeva, performed under the guidance of Pancharatra Agama Praveena Vidwan Sri B.R. Gopal Bhattar, organized by the Devatha family. Maha Annadana followed the ceremonies, and the evening rathotsava carried the Swamy through the village.

All are welcome

There is no registration and no entrance fee: the entire Rampura gramastharu and all devotees are welcome, every day. On festival days, annadana follows the ceremonies.

An open invitation

Everyone is welcome.

There is no registration and no entrance fee. The temple is open every day: mornings for abhisheka, evenings for the deeparadhana. Beyond the festival days, sevas and parihara are performed through the year.
